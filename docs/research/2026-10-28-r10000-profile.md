# R10,000 demo risk profile — 28 October 2026

At R10,000 equity the previous 0.25% per-trade allowance was R25; Farmer was R10 and the total open stop-risk allowance R50. These were account-currency percentages already, not USD amounts. A minimum gold lot with a sufficiently wide valid stop can exceed them.

Updated configured standard per-trade maximum to 0.5% (R50), shared open stop-risk allowance to 1% (R100), and Farmer per-trade/reserved capacity to 0.2% (R20). Keep four account slots, one reserved for Farmer, 2% daily loss threshold (R200 at that baseline), and half sizing after 1% drawdown. Amounts scale with actual equity; no R10,000 balance is assumed by execution. Farmer remains additionally constrained by its converted US$2 maximum loss, target economics and daily US$5 loss limit. The settings are global for this configured bot, not automatically currency-specific.

This explicitly increases allowed risk; currency conversion alone does not make a broker minimum lot cheaper. Four positions are possible only when each lot/stop fits the shared budget and available margin. Stops are not tightened artificially and orders never round volume upward. A hedging account is required for independent same-symbol positions.

Fixed the general strategy cost allowance: cost_buffer_dollars is now converted from USD to account currency too. Existing visible positions retain estimated cost allowances in the shared budget. Missing conversion data blocks execution. Minimum-lot rejections now report account currency, available budget, minimum volume, stop loss, costs and required amount, so the next log can show actual affordability rather than guesswork.

This is not evidence of strategy profitability; stop gaps, slippage and actual fees can exceed estimates and thresholds.
